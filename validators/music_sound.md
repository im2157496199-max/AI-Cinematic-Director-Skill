# Music / Sound Validator

- [ ] Cue has a narrative/emotional function beyond a genre label.
- [ ] Music entry and exit have explicit reasons.
- [ ] Motif reuse changes meaning intentionally when transformed.
- [ ] Anime-style harmony/timbre clichés are not hardcoded as universal defaults.
- [ ] Cultural codes are treated as audience/context-dependent.
- [ ] Music and sound design relationship is intentional: separated, mixed, or unified.
- [ ] Dialogue and important sound effects have protected sonic space.
- [ ] Horizontal state changes and vertical layering are used only when they serve the sequence.
- [ ] Major transitions specify hard cut/cadence/bridge/stinger/silence/pre-lap/etc.
- [ ] Music is not merely reacting one beat late to every edit.
- [ ] Semantic, emotional, temporal, rhythmic, and dialogue/SFX fit are evaluated separately.
- [ ] Short title/OP material has a compact identity arc only when the project needs it.
- [ ] AI generation prompt/spec contains duration, emotion trajectory, structure, transition points, and forbidden clichés.
- [ ] If real OST/audio is supplied, analysis separates transferable patterns from franchise-specific traits.
- [ ] Frontier AI-music papers are treated as reference, not timeless hard rules.
