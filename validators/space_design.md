# Space Design Validator

- [ ] Spatial intent is expressed through topology/enclosure/path/scale, not adjectives only.
- [ ] Important spaces have explicit relationships rather than floating independently.
- [ ] Enclosure strength and opening positions are coherent.
- [ ] Openings separately account for entry/view/light/connection roles when relevant.
- [ ] Main circulation route is distinguishable from secondary routes.
- [ ] Sequence includes purposeful reveal/concealment or transition logic when story needs it.
- [ ] Monumental scale includes readable familiar/human anchors unless abstraction is intentional.
- [ ] Interior scale remains believable relative to doors/stairs/furniture/people.
- [ ] Primary spatial focus is exceptional by size, shape, or placement.
- [ ] Secondary accents do not compete with the primary focus.
- [ ] Repetition creates a family/rhythm; one break may create emphasis.
- [ ] Spatial axis is not confused with the camera 180-degree axis.
- [ ] Light/color decisions are handed to LIGHT_COLOR instead of duplicated here.
- [ ] Art style is handed to Art Direction/reference library instead of inferred from historical architecture examples.
- [ ] Exact engineering/anthropometric/proportion formulas are not invented unless supplied by another source/module.
