# Volume 4 — Character / Concept Design Learnings

The strongest value of pages 130–155 is the visible **decision process**, not just the finished costume.

## Reusable findings
1. **Identity survives variants through a small invariant set.** Hair silhouette, halo, face family and one prop/graphic motif remain stable while clothing category changes radically.
2. **Alternate outfits are not recolors.** Maid, band, swimsuit, dress, New Year, idol, pajamas, uniform and camp variants change material, silhouette and behavior together.
3. **Prop logic changes with the role.** Broom, instrument, float, backpack and formal accessory act as behavior prompts; the weapon remains but no longer owns every composition.
4. **Shape before detail.** Turnarounds and roughs repeatedly solve the outer contour and big layer masses before accessories.
5. **School grammar is a prior, student identity is the override.** Shared colors/symbols create belonging, but each student retains a distinct head silhouette, body rhythm and signature item.
6. **Pair designs need contrast.** Momoi/Midori maid variants demonstrate shared uniform logic while hue, pose and behavioral energy keep the pair separable.
7. **Heavy equipment needs mass compensation.** Hoshino battle and vehicle/weapon concept pages visibly increase stance stability and costume weight when the prop becomes massive.

## AI application
Generate variants in this order: `identity locks -> role/context -> silhouette family -> material/layer changes -> prop behavior -> palette adaptation -> pose`. Do not start by swapping outfit keywords.
