# Volume 4 — PV & Main Story Visual Storytelling

## Sequence-level findings
1. **Establishing information precedes emotional close-up.** City, campus, desert, corridor or transport shots create geography before faces dominate.
2. **Color-state contrast carries story phase.** High-key cyan/white school life, low-key blue/violet threat, magenta/red crisis, then gold/orange release are visibly separated.
3. **Effects need a readable side.** Red-vs-cyan energy, silhouette vs flare and dark mass vs bright blast prevent VFX from becoming visual noise.
4. **Environment becomes an actor during conflict.** Weather, ruins, helicopters, tank wrecks, screens, water and giant machines change the scene's pressure.
5. **Close-ups are earned.** Eye/face close-ups arrive after spatial or action context, so the emotion carries narrative weight.
6. **Rough pages prove composition priority.** Line/animation roughs preserve framing, action direction and value masses long before final rendering.

## AI video use
For an 8-shot sequence, do not generate eight independent pretty images. Define a color state, spatial geography, action direction and emotional escalation, then promote only the strongest frames to hero shots.
