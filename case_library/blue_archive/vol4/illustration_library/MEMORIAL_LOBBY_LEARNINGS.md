# Volume 4 — Memorial Lobby Learnings

Pages 82–119 are treated as environmental character portraits.

## What repeatedly works
- pose is caused by an activity or support surface, not pasted onto the frame
- face is important, but camera height and crop often make torso/legs/prop equally expressive
- one dominant light direction plus controlled fill is more common than flat all-around illumination
- color temperature belongs to the emotional situation: cyan daylight, amber intimacy, blue solitude, pink theatricality
- the room/object context reveals private personality without needing dialogue
- foreshortening is used for closeness and playfulness, while long lenses/large negative space create distance

## AI generation test
Before prompting a lobby-like image, answer: `what is the student doing? what supports their body? where is the primary light? what object proves this is their private moment? what part of the scene may stay visually quiet?`
