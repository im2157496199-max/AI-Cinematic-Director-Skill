# Volume 4 — Illustration & Rough Process

The opening illustration pages and their roughs show a useful production split:
- final promotional art maximizes cast hierarchy, event identity and emotional hook
- rough pages expose the hidden structure: figure grouping, horizon, major diagonals, empty zones and color-mass planning

For AI image work, preserve the rough-stage decisions before adding detail. If a prompt rewrite changes the subject hierarchy or main diagonal, it is no longer a "detail refinement"; it is a new composition.
