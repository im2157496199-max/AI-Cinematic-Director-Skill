# Volume 4 — Light, Color, Composition & Scene Learnings

## Memorial Lobby pattern
The scene is never "background + character". The environment supplies a private activity, physical support, time of day and emotional temperature. Beds, stairs, rooftops, bars, windows, desks, instruments and phones give the pose a reason to exist.

### Light families
- **High-key daylight:** white/cyan bounce, soft sky light, low shadow density. Best for openness, youth, summer and ordinary school life.
- **Warm practical/interior light:** amber lamps, sunset or bar/cafe sources against darker rooms. Best for intimacy, maturity and memory.
- **Cool low-key night:** blue/violet windows/screens with isolated warm points. Best for solitude, control, anxiety or quiet.
- **Back/rim light:** window/sunset light separates hair/halo and lets silhouettes read before detail.

### Composition families
- strong diagonals for playful/dynamic portraits
- large negative space for solitude or aftermath
- foreground props for intimacy and depth
- repeated architecture (stairs, rails, desks, windows) for perspective rhythm
- character off-center when the environment carries narrative information

## PV / Main Story color-script pattern
Bright cyan school-life passages and low-key violet/red conflict passages are kept visibly distinct. Warm golden sequences are used as emotional release/closure. The important rule is **state contrast across a sequence**, not a fixed color-symbol dictionary.

## Environment design
Kivotos spaces feel designed because signage, transit, fences, club rooms, school corridors and local cultural motifs are functional first. Small ordinary details sell the world more reliably than decorative clutter.
