# Memorial Lobby learnings — Volume 3

Reviewed final Memorial Lobby pages p.080–122 and rough/development pages p.123–130.

## Repeating visual strategy
- The character is rarely a neutral standing model; a physical task or everyday prop gives the pose meaning.
- Emotional proximity is prioritized over grand spectacle. Many images use medium-close or intimate framing.
- Light usually has one readable emotional job: warm domestic comfort, cool solitude, sunset nostalgia, moonlit quiet, or vivid cyber energy.
- Background detail is selective. The environment supports the character's situation rather than competing with the face.
- Strong cases use an asymmetrical composition and leave breathing room in the direction of gaze or body movement.
- Color is usually organized around 2–3 dominant families, with halo/eyes/small accessories acting as accent points.

## Rough-page lesson
The roughs show that value structure, camera angle, crop, body direction and prop relation are solved before polish. For AI workflows, composition selection should therefore happen before detail prompting.
