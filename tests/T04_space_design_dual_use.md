# T04 — Space Design Dual-Use Test

## Case A — Anime/Game Megastructure
Intent: a young character enters a civilization-scale underground city.

Expected:
- monumental/mixed scale
- at least one human/familiar anchor
- approach -> threshold -> dominant volume
- repeated structural family to make scale measurable
- dominant space distinguished by size/placement
- reveal sequence rather than showing everything at once
- no forced historical architectural style

Failure:
- only adds `gigantic`, `epic`, `massive`
- no scale anchor
- random architecture with no route or dominant space

## Case B — Furniture / Interior Visualization
Intent: apartment layout + cabinet/furniture placement for marketing visualization.

Expected:
- adjacency/circulation first
- rooms linked in believable sequence
- human/furniture scale anchors
- enclosure and view openings respected
- dominant presentation zones identified
- no fabrication-grade dimensions unless provided

Failure:
- each room treated as an isolated beauty shot
- circulation blocked by furniture
- impossible/ambiguous room relationships
