# Color and Light — James Gurney — Focused / Deduplicated Skill Extraction

> Use this source for transferable light/color/perception rules, not for its painterly taste.
> Target media: anime, manga, games, film, AI images/video, interiors.
> Labels: NEW_ACTIVE / MERGE_EXISTING / REFERENCE_ONLY / AI_ADAPTATION.

## NEW_ACTIVE
G01 Light-source model: source type, direction, relative intensity, hardness, color, coverage, visibility.
G02 Keep primary/secondary/bounce illumination hierarchical unless deliberately stylized.
G03 Keep light-family and shadow-family coherent; do not brighten surfaces independently.
G04 Use small contact/occlusion darks where forms crowd out light.
G05 Lighting can allocate attention; local illumination may raise salience.
G06 Color is relational; judge colors inside neighboring colors and illumination.
G07 Use restricted gamut to create unity; reserve unusual/high-chroma colors for emphasis.
G08 Accent color is limited: if accents are everywhere, no accent remains.
G09 Warm/cool is relative; do not hard-code universal emotional meanings.
G10 Colored lights interact additively; overlapping light and blocked-source shadows change color.
G11 Materials need distinct light response: diffuse, glossy, reflective, translucent, subsurface, emissive.
G12 Subsurface scattering is selective and geometry/light dependent.
G13 Specular reflection should reflect compatible surrounding environment.
G14 Atmospheric depth modifies contrast, saturation, hue and edge clarity with distance.
G15 Fog/mist/smoke/dust/light shafts are volumetric depth/mood variables, not decoration only.
G16 Controlled gradation can prevent flat synthetic color fields.
G17 Neutrals protect saturated/high-chroma moments from competition.
G18 Color Script belongs at sequence level: adjacent panels/frames should be judged together.
G19 Palette shifts can signal location, time, reveal, danger, dream, emotional or act transitions.
G20 Style and physics are separate controls: anime/game/cel-shaded/graphic/photoreal can share coherent light logic.
