# Anime Persona Trends Core

Purpose:
Use empirical anime-character research as a validator, not as a replacement for design judgment.

## What this layer is for
- checking whether trait bundles have visible support
- balancing readability with novelty
- remembering that visual design strongly affects attachment and recognition

## Active rules

1. Treat characters as multimodal.
   - personality, visual signals, role context and performance cues all matter.

2. Use archetype clusters as broad priors only.
   - Energetic Extrovert, Reserved Introvert, Arrogant Tsundere etc. are pattern reminders.

3. Decide whether you want convention or deviation.
   - some types benefit from familiar tropes for immediate readability.
   - others should strategically violate expectation to gain memorability.

4. Strong visual signals are mandatory in image-first workflows.
   - face, silhouette, hair mass, prop identity and local focal contrast often matter more than abstract biography.

5. A character should survive outside story context.
   - if removed from scene narrative, can the character still be recognized by shape/color/behavior cue?

6. Psychological complexity should have visible form.
   - contradiction needs body-language, spacing, expression-control or costume-attitude evidence.

7. Avoid trend obedience.
   - historical moe-ification is context, not destiny.

## Preferred AI check questions
- Is the intended trait bundle visually inferable?
- Is the design too trope-flat?
- What is the one familiar anchor and the one differentiating twist?
- Does popularity-seeking simplification erase role/story truth?
